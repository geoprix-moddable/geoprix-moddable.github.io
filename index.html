Why moddable?

Plug and play

Mods are single .gpmod files. Copy one into the GeoPrix Mods folder and switch it on.

Turn on and off

Every mod has its own switch in the Mods screen. One restart and it's in or out.

No root needed

The mod loader is built into the game. Mods go in a normal folder on your phone.
