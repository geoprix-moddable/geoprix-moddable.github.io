About

GeoPrix Moddable (game version 3.3) loads Java mods when the game starts. Mods can add buttons to the Settings tab, keep their own saved data, call game classes and, on a real arm64 phone, hook game methods.

Mods

Custom Cars

Put a team's own image on its car, or replace the car with it.

Team Logos

Show an uploaded image for a team instead of its colour.

Engine Sound

Every car in a race gets its own looping engine sound.

Satellite Race

Races and highlight videos use the satellite map instead of the street map.

Sub-Stats Unlock

Shows driver and car sub-stats on the team and driver sheets without Pro.

Hello Screen

Example mod: a Settings button that opens a dialog. Start here to make your own.

Install

  1. Install the GeoPrix Moddable APK on your phone.
  2. Download a .gpmod file.
  3. Copy it into the GeoPrix Mods folder with the Files app.
  4. Restart GeoPrix.
