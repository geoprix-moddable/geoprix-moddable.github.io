Browse mods How to install

Why moddable?

Plug and play

Mods are single .gpmod files. Copy one into the GeoPrix Mods folder and switch it on.

Turn on and off

Every mod has its own switch in the Mods screen. One restart and it's in or out.

Make your own

Mods are Java. Copy the example, change the code, build it with one command.

Featured mods

Custom Cars

Put a team's own image on its car, or swap the car for it completely.

Team Logos

Each team can show an uploaded logo instead of its colour.

Engine Sound

Every car in a race gets its own engine sound.
