Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who we are

GeoPrix Moddable is a fan project that adds a mod loader to the game GeoPrix. This website is geoprix-moddable.github.io. It is not made by or connected to the developer of GeoPrix.

What we collect

Most of the site works without an account and we don't collect anything about you. If you create a forum account, we store:

  • your email address (to sign you in, confirm your account and reset your password);
  • your username, which everyone can see next to your posts;
  • the threads and replies you post, which are public;
  • if you use Continue with Google: the name and email address Google shares with us. We don't get your Google password or anything else from your Google account.

How we use it

Only to run the forum: to sign you in, show your username on your posts, and stop spam and abuse. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use it for tracking or marketing. We don't send newsletters.

Where it is stored

Forum accounts and posts are stored with Supabase (servers in the EU, Ireland). The website itself is hosted by GitHub Pages, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts; both may log your IP address as part of serving the site.

Cookies and storage

When you sign in, your browser keeps a sign-in token in local storage so you stay signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • You can delete your own threads and replies at any time.
  • To delete your account and everything linked to it, post in the forum's Help category or ask an admin. We'll delete it within 30 days.
  • If you signed in with Google, you can also remove our access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Children

The forum is not meant for children under 13. Don't create an account if you are under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
