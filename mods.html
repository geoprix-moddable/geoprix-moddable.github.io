Mods

All mods are for GeoPrix 3.3. Turn each one on in Settings > MODS, then tap RESTART.

Emulator OK means the mod works fully on an Android emulator. Mods marked Full features need a phone use method hooks for part of what they do, which only run on a real arm64 phone.

Custom Cars

geoprix.customcars

Put a team's own image on its car, or swap the car for it completely.

  • Custom car image switch and preview on the team sheet
  • Editor: drag to move, pinch to resize, twist to rotate
  • Size, rotation, flip and centre controls
  • Option to hide the game's car and show only your image
  • Works in live races and everywhere the game draws a car
v1.6Emulator OK

Team Logos

geoprix.teamlogos

Each team can show an uploaded logo instead of its colour.

  • Logo toggle and picker on the team sheet
  • Logos in the grid, race tower and race results
  • Logos in rendered highlight reels
  • Logos travel with shared .geoprix grid files
v2.3Emulator OK

Engine Sound

geoprix.enginesound

Every car in a race gets its own engine sound.

  • Pitch follows each car's speed and the race speed
  • Louder when zoomed in, panned by where the car is on screen
  • Silent when paused or in the background
  • Rendered highlight videos get engine audio (phones only)
  • ENGINE SOUND on/off button in Settings
v1.3Full features need a phone

Satellite Race

example.satelliterace

Races and highlight videos use the satellite map instead of the street map.

  • Uses the game's own satellite imagery
  • Works on races, highlights and reels
  • Turn it on in the Mods screen, then restart
v1.1Emulator OK

Sub-Stats Unlock

geoprix.substatsunlock

Edit driver and car sub-stats on the team and driver sheets without Pro.

  • Driver pace, quali, overtaking, defending, consistency
  • Car power and downforce
  • Free races up to 80 laps (normally 20)
  • Free seasons up to 30 races (normally 8)
v1.1Emulator OK

Hello Screen

example.helloscreen

Example mod: a Settings button that opens a dialog. Start here to make your own.

  • Adds a HELLO SCREEN button to Settings
  • Shows text from the mod's own assets/hello.txt
  • The smallest working mod, made to be copied
v1.0Emulator OK
